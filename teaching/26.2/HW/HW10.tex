  Show that $G/H\simeq K$ and $G/K\simeq H$.

\item
   Give a composition series for the following groups:  $\ZZ_{30}$, $D_{30}$, $A_{30}$, $S_{30}$.
 
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%


%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\item[$\bullet$]
  A group $G$ is \demph{solvable} if it has a subnormal series
  \[
  \{e\}=H_0\ \triangleleft\ H_1\ \triangleleft\ \dotsb\ \triangleleft\ H_{n-1}\  \triangleleft\ H_n= G
  \]
  in which every subquotient $H_i/H_{i-1}$ is abelian.
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%


%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\item
  Prove that a subgroup $K$ of a solvable group is solvable.\newline
  (Hint: Consider the composition $K\cap H_{i+1}\to H_{i+1} \twoheadrightarrow  H_{i+1}/H_i$.)
  
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%



%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\item
  Suppose that $H$ and $K$ are solvable groups.
  Prove that $H\times K$ is solvable.
